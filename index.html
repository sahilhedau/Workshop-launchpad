Viral Loop Mechanics ($K$-Factor)
Viral Coefficient $K = \text{Share Rate} \times \text{Signups Per Sharer} = 0.25 \times 1.2 = \mathbf{0.30}$. A $K$ of 0.30 amplifies every 100 direct registrations with 30 additional referral sign-ups. Because $K < 1$, the loop is stable and realistic (sub-exponential viral decay), yielding exactly 117 incremental registrations on top of the 390 base.

How Attribution Tracking Works in Workshop Launchpad
1. A connector shares a link: register.html?ref=ADI-7KQ&src=wa.
2. When a student opens the page, an HTTP click event is tracked for that code.
3. When the student submits the form, the registration is credited to ADI-7KQ, the WhatsApp channel, and today's pacing total.
4. A unique personal referral link is generated for the student to invite classmates, powering Channel C!
